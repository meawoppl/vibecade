02 / Come out and play

Walk up.
Join in.

No app to install. No tickets. Free to play, always.

  1. Find the glow

    When we're open, come to 2051 Harrison St and look for the windows playing games.

  2. Scan and connect

    Scan the QR code in the window and follow the steps to join the guest Wi-Fi. Your controller opens in the browser.

  3. Thumbs ready

    Your phone becomes the pad. Crowd games welcome up to 24 players, and turns rotate when there's a queue.

Still putting the pieces together. A two-window pilot has been running and we're building toward all six. There's no opening date yet; this page will say when the windows are on.

04 / The build

A building-sized
little experiment.

Six ordinary windows, each about 49 inches square, in two groups of three either side of the door.

  • 6projectors, one behind each pane, shining onto a screen on the glass
  • 1small computer behind every projector
  • 1program, written in Rust, running every game
  • 0apps to install: your phone's browser is the controller
