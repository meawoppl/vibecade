02 / THE BUILD

A building-sized
little experiment.

Six ordinary windows.
A new way to play together.

The plan: six windows, each about 49 inches square, in two groups of three on either side of the door. One projector per pane shines from behind the glass, with a small computer behind each.

One Rust program runs the games. Your phone handles the buttons. The windows do the rest.
